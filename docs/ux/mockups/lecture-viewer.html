Faculty notes · slide 6

Visible in instructor mode only. Never shown in the student build.

Say

A requirement is not the need rewritten with "shall". Ask the room what changed between NEED-007 and SR-012: the time window, the measure, the location and the threshold.

Then point out that SR-012 still says nothing about the aircraft. That only happens at SYS-031, after allocation.

Ask (1 min, think–pair–share)

Who could verify SR-012, and how? Expect "test at the nearest home"; push for who owns the measurement.

Watch for

  • Students calling SYS-031 a stakeholder requirement.
  • The 60 dBA value being read as a real limit; it is a teaching value.

Source

INCOSE Needs and Requirements Manual, the transformation of needs into requirements (page reference held in the lecture source).
