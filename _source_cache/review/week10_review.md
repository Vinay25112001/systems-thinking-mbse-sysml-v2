# Week 10 independent review — SysML v2 requirements and structure (L10, model v0.2, prepares CP4)

Reviewer: independent adversarial reviewer (AI, Claude), 2026-10-11. No course or app file was edited.
Scope: `src/content/weeks/week10.ts`, `src/activities/week10.activity.tsx`, `src/activities/week10/*`, `sysml/week10/*.sysml`, `sysml/instructor/week10/*`, `docs/authoring/week10_notes.md`. ## Verdict

**REVISE (moderate). Not ready for sign-off.** The SysML core is sound. Every listing is validated with a SHA-matched record, the citations are almost all faithful, and the satisfy-as-assertion thread is taught honestly. The defects that remain are these:

- The predict activity leaks its key before the reveal (F01).
- The translator lets two realistic bad inputs pass every check, against rule 5 (F02, F03).
- The subject-first caution is wrong, and my probe settles it (F04).
- The graduate answer key and the still-air example overclaim what their assumptions achieve (F05, F06).
- The defence of the REQ-SYS-010 satisfy is not what the model says (F07).
- q10-7 contradicts the worked examples and the reference (F08).
- The quiz key is visibly biased (F09).
- The graduate memo and the in-class requirements step are under-budgeted (F10, F11).

Tests: `npx vitest run src/activities/week10 src/content src/lib` gave **105/105 pass**, and `npx tsc -b` was **clean**. The instructor key is excluded from the bundle: the glob `'!../../sysml/instructor/**'` is in `src/lib/sysml.ts` line 5, and the test "nothing under sysml/instructor/ is bundled into the app" passes. All 16 Week 10 files (15 + the instructor key) match their records in `sysml/validation-report.json` by SHA-256.
