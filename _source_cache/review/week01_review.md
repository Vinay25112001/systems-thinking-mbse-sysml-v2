## Summary verdict: **PASS WITH FIXES** (the major findings must be closed before the week is marked `independently-reviewed`)

The citations are unusually faithful. All 33 source citations land on the cited page, and the one block quotation is exact. The systems-thinking concepts are correct, and the session plan, workload and structural checks are all sound.

The defects are concentrated in the **claims about the AeroLink dynamics explorer**:

- The "plateau at the charging ceiling" does not happen. Served trips settle at about 90 % of the ceiling, and no scenario ever has unmet demand.
- The explorer's "binding constraint" label is wrong whenever the system is demand-limited.
- "Growth and Underinvestment" is claimed but the model does not reproduce it.
- The causal-loop diagram omits a loop that the model contains.
- One "strong" leverage answer in the key cannot be tested in the explorer.
- The answer key says these claims are "test-asserted", but one of them is not, and the relevant test cannot fail.

On the assessment side:

- The quiz is mostly at Understand level, against the authoring guide.
- Three quiz items carry the wrong objective tag, and nothing tests loop polarity.
- The graduate memo may never be read under the RB-16 sampling rule.

**Counts:** critical 0 · major 11 · minor 14.
