## Verdict: **PASS WITH FIXES**

No critical defects. Citation fidelity is high: every page reference lands on the right page, and no quote is fabricated. But eight major defects need fixing before release:
- a boundary contradiction with Week 1 (ENT-001);
- an incomplete operational-independence argument in the central B2.1 worked example;
- AROU IDs in the answer key that collide with the reference model;
- a reference model that fails its own L2 coverage rule;
- a "generic concern" check that does nothing for any statement of five or more words;
- a graduate memo that the RB-16 sampling rule may never grade;
- an implausible 0.6 h workload line for the memo;
- a recorder that cannot apply the AI-suggested-reviewed tag that the module requires.

Counts: **critical 0 · major 8 · minor 20**.
