# Academic Quality Review

**Status:** record of the independent quality review of the curriculum package, 2026-10-09.

**What this is not.** It is not a Wright State review, an accreditation review or a faculty endorsement. The reviewer was an **independent AI reviewer** (a separate agent instance, which did not author the documents), briefed to be adversarial. Faculty review is still required.

Full reviewer reports (verbatim):
- `_source_cache/review/external_review_round1.md`

## 1. Method

| Round | What the reviewer did | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Round 1 | Read all eight drafts, the four official syllabus texts and the source extracts. Re-computed every weight table, workload series and page count, and spot-checked **42 claims** against the source pages. | Not allowed to modify any project file. |
| Author revision | Revised every finding. The evidence maps, the Bloom table and the reading pages are now **generated by scripts** rather than maintained by hand. | — |
| Round 2 | Re-checked every disposition, re-ran all four scripts, spot-checked **28 claims**, and re-rated. | Not allowed to modify any project file. |
| Author clean-up | Fixed the 11 new minor findings (N1–N11) and strengthened the checker to catch N1/N2-type faults. | This clean-up has **not** been re-reviewed (round 3 not run). |

**Rating scale (0–4):**

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Absent or unacceptable |
| 1 | Major deficiencies |
| 2 | Adequate, with significant gaps |
| 3 | Good, with minor gaps |
| 4 | Exemplary, ready |

**Capping rules applied by the reviewer:**
- A criterion is capped at 2 if a verified error of that kind is assessed or would mislead students.
- Readiness is capped at 2 while any critical finding is open.
- Round 2 added: laboratory feasibility is capped at 2 until a SysML tool has been run on the instructor reference model.

## 4. Remaining critical deficiencies and the conditions for approval

There is **no open critical finding**. **These conditions must be met before either pathway can be approved to run:**

1. **FD-08: SysML tool.** Evaluate at least two candidates locally against the protocol. Build and time the instructor reference model. Only then do the lab-feasibility ratings and the SysML-graded instruments become real.
2. **FD-27: language baseline.** Obtain the formal SysML 2.0 specification, if approved, or accept the beta text with that limitation stated.
3. **FD-20: accessibility.** Screen-reader evaluation of the tool; the university's standard accessibility statement.
4. **FD-17: individual accountability.** Confirm conjunctive vs individual-only attainment. Confirm the required individual items for A2.
5. **SE-methods gaps (B rating 2).** Configuration management and life-cycle cost are absent, and integration and security are at awareness depth only. Faculty should either accept these gaps for Pathway B or fund a 4-credit version (FD-02).

## 5. Reviewer limitations, stated honestly

- The reviewer is an AI agent, not a faculty member or an accreditation body. Its ratings are structured critique, not an institutional judgement.
- Both reviewer and author worked from the same extracted text. A source error present in the text layer would be shared by both.
- The clean-up after round 2 is unreviewed. A round-3 check is recommended after faculty comments.
- About 23 % of the source collection was never opened (`SOURCE_INVENTORY.md` §2). No assigned body page lies in that portion.
