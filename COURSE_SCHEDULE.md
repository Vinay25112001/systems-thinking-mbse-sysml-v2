## Instructional rules (faculty direction, 2026-10-18)

1. Each student team develops its own enterprise, approved by the instructor in Week 1.
2. AeroLink Urban Mobility is the instructor's continuous worked example, not the project every team must do.
3. Each week first explains the concept with the AeroLink example, then guides students to apply the method to their own enterprise.
4. The Week 7 output (the Systems Thinking Engineering Package) is the source material for Phase II.
5. The Week 14 output is a coherent SysML v2 model developed from the team's own package.
6. Continuity from document-based to model-based engineering is explicit: every Phase II week names the Phase I artifact it models (Week 8 ↔ Week 1, Week 9 ↔ Week 2, Week 10 ↔ Weeks 2 and 4, Week 11 ↔ Week 5, Week 12 ↔ Weeks 2 and 3, Week 13 ↔ Week 3, Week 14 ↔ Week 6 and Week 7).
7. Introductory MBSE and SysML v2 material sits in Week 8. No week is added and no whiteboard topic is displaced.
8. Professional American English across the course, starting with the welcome page and Week 1.
9. Weekly navigation: Overview → Learn → Practice → Check & Submit.
10. One complete lesson at a time. Week 1 is the design template and is approved before Weeks 2–14 are rebuilt.

## What every week contains

Learning objectives; source-grounded instruction; clear explanations; an AeroLink worked example; a guided interactive
exercise; a student enterprise activity; an engineering deliverable; a knowledge assessment; a grading rubric; required
source references; and a workload estimate. Weekly deliverables build the same enterprise package and then the same
model. No unrelated assignments.
